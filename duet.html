The Duo

Ayanna Rose sings lead, Eddie Aguilar plays piano and sings — a piano/vocal duo built for cocktail hours, lounges, dinners, and events. Behind the two of them sits a full-band sound: Eddie produces custom-made backing tracks for the act, so the duo can carry everything from jazz standards and ballads to dance-floor favorites.
