Ayanna Rose

Lead vocals

Ayanna Rose is the duo's front voice — a lead vocalist who carries the room from the first dinner standard to the last dance. Her featured performances front full live bands at ballroom and corporate events, and in the duo she trades leads and harmonies with Eddie across the whole book: soul, R&B, pop, jazz standards and contemporary dance.

Warm on the mic and quick with a request, she gives the act its face — the singer guests ask for by name on night two.

Eddie Aguilar

Piano · Vocals · Musical Director

Eddie Aguilar is a professional bassist, vocalist, pianist, guitarist and bandleader with more than twenty years on stage. In the duo he anchors from the piano — singing lead and harmony, running the custom backing tracks and directing the night like the bandleader he is.

As bandleader for Downtown Fever, managed by Emerald City, he hired players, built sets and kept dance floors full across Dallas's corporate and wedding circuit. He sight-reads, sings in English and Spanish, and brings that same reliability to every residency set.
