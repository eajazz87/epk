The Reading Chair

Eddie Aguilar is first and foremost a bassist — 20+ years of professional experience, and a sight-reader. Hand him a chart and the show runs: show band, pit, and reading-chair work is his home ground. In Dallas he led Downtown Fever (managed by Emerald City) as bandleader and has performed with Icehouse Band, The Groove Line, Jordan Kahn Music Company, Random Axis, Stratosphere, and Aura by Scott Michaels.
