The musician

Versatility with a bandleader’s instincts.

Eddie Aguilar is a professional bassist, vocalist, pianist, guitarist and bandleader with more than two decades of live experience. Now based in Bangkok and available internationally, he is focused on hotel and resort residencies — as a soloist, with vocalist Ayanna Rose as the duo Eddie Aguilar & Ayanna Rose, and fronting a full band.

As bandleader for Downtown Fever, managed by Emerald City, Eddie combined musical direction with the practical work that makes an event run: hiring players, reading the room, shaping the set and keeping the floor moving.

His musical range crosses jazz, soul, R&B, rock, country and contemporary dance music. He sight-reads, sings in English and Spanish, and brings a bandleader’s reliability to residency programming: multiple sets a night, dinner to dance floor, week after week. Offstage, his teaching experience and home recording setup support private bass instruction, remote sessions and custom production work.
