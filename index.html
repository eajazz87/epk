About

Eddie Aguilar is a professional bassist with 20+ years of experience and a veteran of the Dallas music scene. As bandleader for Downtown Fever (managed by Emerald City), he fronted one of the region's top corporate and event bands, and he has also performed with Icehouse Band, The Groove Line, Jordan Kahn Music Company, Random Axis, Stratosphere, and Aura by Scott Michaels.

Off stage, Eddie has hired hundreds of musicians and books artists and bands for a Dallas club, coordinating client events and weddings. He reads music (sight-reading), sings, and also plays piano and guitar. His genres run jazz, soul, R&B, rock, and country. Before the band years, he taught trombone, euphonium, and tuba to high school students for Irving ISD.
